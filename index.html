The Interactive Music of Hyrule Field

This project recreates and visualizes the pioneering interactive soundtrack of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998). Powered by authentic Nintendo 64 soundfont instruments and real-time Web Audio synthesis, every orchestral voice is performed live in your browser.

Composer Koji Kondo designed Hyrule Field as video game history's landmark dynamic score. Rather than playing a looping recording, the music branches across modular 8-measure phrases that adapt to player actions—transitioning smoothly between energetic running themes, tense battle fanfares when enemies draw near, and serene campfire rest melodies.

When resting, the heroic brass melody fades to leave gentle pastoral harp chords. Use the controls below to steer the musical path between Run, Rest, and Battle, or turn on Autoplay to let the score evolve on its own.
